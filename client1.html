Global Manufacturing Inc.

Thermal Fluid System Installation & Maintenance

Project Overview

We partnered with Global Manufacturing Inc. to deliver a comprehensive thermal fluid system installation for their primary high-volume production line. The project required precision engineering to ensure consistent temperature control, which is critical for their manufacturing process.

Our team managed the entire lifecycle of the project, from initial system design and component selection to installation and final commissioning. Post-installation, we entered into an ongoing maintenance contract to ensure the system operates at peak efficiency.

Key Achievements

  • Successful installation of a 2M Kcal/hr thermal fluid heater.
  • Implementation of automated temperature control systems reducing energy waste by 15%.
  • Zero unplanned downtime recorded over the last 12 months of maintenance.
